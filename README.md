# 🏥 Healthcare Analytics Dashboard (Excel)

## 📌 Project Overview
This project is an interactive **Healthcare Analytics Dashboard** built in Microsoft Excel. The main objective is to analyze operational, clinical, and financial performance across a healthcare network (**Nile Care**). 

By transforming raw hospital data into meaningful visualizations and key performance indicators (KPIs), this dashboard enables healthcare managers and executive teams to monitor patient trends, optimize branch efficiency, and evaluate profitability.

---

## 📊 Key Insights & Highlights

- **Overall Performance:** Analyzed **17,873 patient visits** across **8 branches**, generating **EGP 7.4M in total revenue** and **EGP 4.27M in total profit** (Net Profit Margin of ~57.4%).
- **Branch Performance:** **Nile Care Nasr City** led in total patient visits (**3,041 visits**), followed by **Heliopolis** (**2,835 visits**) and **6th of October** (**2,767 visits**).
- **Financial Breakdown:** Analyzed profit margins across medical specialties—**Cardiology** generated the highest profit (**EGP 955K**), followed by **Orthopedics** (**EGP 556K**) and **Dermatology** (**EGP 427K**).
- **Operational Metrics:** 
  - **Average Waiting Time:** Monitored waiting times per branch to improve service efficiency (Overall Network Average: **~24-25 minutes**).
  - **Consultation Duration:** Tracked average doctor-patient interaction time (Average: **~19 minutes**).
- **Doctor & Medical Performance:**
  - Tracked top performing physicians by patient volume (Led by **Dr. Megahed Ahmed** with **970 visits**).
  - **Lab Request Rate:** **~20.4%** of visits required laboratory testing (3,652 total lab orders).
  - **Revisit & Follow-Up Rate:** Analyzed 30-day patient revisit rates (**17.3%** / 3,095 revisits) to ensure continuity of care.
- **Insurance Demographics:** Visualized patient payment channels—**Corporate** and **Private** insurances accounted for the largest share of patient visits.
